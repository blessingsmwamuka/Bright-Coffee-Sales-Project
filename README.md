 Bright Coffee Shop – Sales Analysis Project

 1. Project Overview

The **Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Project** focuses on analysing historical transactional sales data to generate meaningful business insights and support strategic decision-making.

The project was developed to assist the newly appointed CEO of Bright Coffee Shop in understanding the company's sales performance, identifying revenue-generating products, evaluating sales patterns and improving overall business operations.

By applying data analytics, SQL querying and data visualisation techniques, the project transforms raw transactional data into meaningful information that can support business planning, product strategy and operational improvements.

The analysis follows a structured data analytics workflow, beginning with project planning and data preparation, followed by data exploration, visualisation and the development of actionable business recommendations.

2. Project Objectives

The primary objective is to use data analytics and visualisation to evaluate Bright Coffee Shop's sales performance and provide actionable recommendations.

The specific objectives are to:

- Identify products that contribute most significantly to overall revenue.
- Analyse sales performance across different times of the day.
- Examine sales patterns across product categories and time intervals.
- Identify trends that could influence business performance.
- Develop interactive visualisations to communicate analytical findings.
- Provide data-driven recommendations to improve revenue and customer engagement.

3. Business Questions

The analysis is guided by the following business questions:

1. Which products generate the most revenue?
2. What time of day does the store perform best?
3. What sales trends exist across different product types and time intervals?
4. Which product categories demonstrate stronger sales performance?
5. What recommendations can improve sales performance and customer engagement?

These questions provide direction for the SQL queries, Excel analysis and dashboard development.

4. Tools and Technologies

The project uses the following technologies:

Data Processing and Analysis
- **SQL:** Querying, cleaning, transforming and analysing transactional data.
- **Databricks:** Loading and processing the dataset and performing analytical operations.

Data Analysis and Visualisation
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data analysis, pivot tables and graphical representations.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Developing interactive dashboards and presenting business insights.
- **Google Looker Studio:** Dashboard development and data visualisation, where applicable.

Project Planning and Presentation
- **Miro:** Designing the project workflow and data architecture.
- **Microsoft PowerPoint:** Presenting project findings and recommendations.
- **Canva:** Supporting the visual design and presentation of results.

5. Project Methodology

The project follows a structured data analytics process consisting of the following stages.

Task 1: Project Planning and Architecture

The initial stage focused on establishing the project's objectives, scope and analytical requirements.

Activities included:
- Defining the business problem and project objectives.
- Identifying the key business questions.
- Developing the project workflow.
- Designing the data flow and ETL architecture using Miro.
- Establishing the analytical tasks and expected deliverables.

Task 2: Data Preparation and Processing

The second stage involved preparing the original dataset for analysis.

Activities included:
- Converting the original Excel dataset into CSV format.
- Loading the dataset into Databricks.
- Examining the dataset structure and identifying relevant fields.
- Cleaning and transforming the data.
- Preparing the dataset for SQL-based analysis.

Task 3: SQL Data Analysis

SQL was used to investigate the transactional dataset and answer the identified business questions.

The analysis focused on:
- Examining transaction records.
- Analysing product performance.
- Investigating sales across different time intervals.
- Evaluating sales performance across product categories.
- Organising analytical results for further interpretation and visualisation.

Task 4: Excel Analysis and Visualisation

Microsoft Excel was used to summarise and visualise relevant information from the dataset.

Activities included:
- Creating pivot tables to summarise sales information.
- Comparing product and category performance.
- Developing charts to communicate sales patterns.
- Using visual representations to identify important trends.

Task 5: Dashboard Development

Dashboard development focused on presenting the analytical findings through accessible and interactive visualisations.

The project uses data visualisation tools to:
- Present important sales performance indicators.
- Compare product and category performance.
- Display sales patterns across different time intervals.
- Improve the accessibility and interpretation of analytical findings.
- Support business monitoring and decision-making.

Task 6: Interpretation and Business Recommendations

The final analytical stage involved interpreting the results and translating the findings into practical business recommendations.

Activities included:
- Reviewing the SQL analysis and visualisations.
- Identifying important sales trends.
- Evaluating product and category performance.
- Developing recommendations based on the analytical findings.
- Preparing the final project presentation.
